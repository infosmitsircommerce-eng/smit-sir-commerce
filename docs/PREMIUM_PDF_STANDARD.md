# Smit Sir Commerce — Premium Master PDF Standard

This document is the production standard for future Premium PDFs.

## 1. The benchmark

The current benchmark is the **CBSE Class 12 Accountancy — Financial Ratios: Analysis** Premium Master sample.

A future Premium PDF must not be advertised as matching the Premium Master standard unless it follows the requirements below.

## 2. Visual standard

- A4 portrait by default.
- Bright, premium light theme; no dark full-page reading backgrounds.
- Large, mobile-readable typography.
- Strong page hierarchy: chapter label → page title → concept blocks → worked logic → takeaway.
- Generous whitespace; avoid dense textbook walls.
- Consistent palette based on warm ivory, deep ink/navy, teal/sage, muted gold and restrained lime accents.
- Diagrams, formula cards and worked examples must be visually designed rather than placed as plain paragraphs.
- No tiny footnotes used to hide important learning content.
- No stretched, blurry or heavily compressed page artwork.

## 3. Learning structure

Every major concept should answer:

1. What does this concept mean?
2. Why is it used?
3. What figures belong in the formula or rule?
4. How is it solved or applied?
5. What common mistake should the student avoid?
6. How should the final answer be presented in an exam?

Preferred recurring modules:

- **Smit Sir’s Logic**
- **Formula**
- **Worked Example**
- **Exam Tip**
- **Common Trap**
- **Teacher POV**
- **Final Answer**
- **Interpretation**
- **Quick Revision**

## 4. Numerical pages

Worked numericals must show the method, not only the answer.

Minimum pattern:

- Question
- Formula / governing equation
- Substitution
- Working
- Final answer
- Correct unit / ratio format
- One exam or classification note where useful

Reverse problems should translate the English information into a basic equation before calculation.

## 5. Readability

- Body text must remain comfortable on a phone without aggressive zooming.
- Important formulas and final answers should be substantially larger than body text.
- Avoid more than 2–3 dense columns on A4.
- Tables must remain legible after ordinary PDF rendering.
- A page should teach one main idea or one clear worked sequence.

## 6. Quality control before publishing

Before a Premium Master PDF is published:

- Verify educational content against the approved source notes.
- Render the complete PDF and inspect representative pages.
- Check for clipping, overlap, broken glyphs, blurry images and inconsistent margins.
- Verify page count and file integrity.
- Test the PDF on desktop and mobile.
- Keep a free sample or representative preview where commercially appropriate.
- Do not list a protected PDF as ready until its actual file is synced and opens through the verified-access flow.

## 7. Website promise

The website should distinguish clearly between:

- **FREE:** public notes, public tools and full samples explicitly marked free.
- **LIVE PREMIUM:** protected resources whose actual files are synced and verified.
- **UPGRADING:** catalogue items being rebuilt to this standard and not yet represented as ready protected PDFs.

The Financial Ratios sample is the reference quality bar for Accountancy Premium rebuilds.
