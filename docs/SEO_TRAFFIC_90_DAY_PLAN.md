# Smit Sir Commerce — Organic Traffic Operating Plan

This plan prioritises genuine search demand, useful learning content and stable indexing over mass-generated pages or backlink schemes.

## Core traffic pillars

### 1. Commerce study resources
Keep the strongest free resources easy to crawl from `/study-material`, subject hubs, chapter pages and related practice pages. Every important page should have a unique title, description, H1, canonical URL and useful internal links.

### 2. UGC NET / GSET Commerce
The competitive-exam cluster is built around one shared 43-PDF library instead of duplicating the same notes across two exam sites.

Primary hubs:
- `/net-gset-commerce`
- `/ugc-net-commerce-notes`
- `/gset-commerce-code-17-notes`

Ten unit landing pages connect search intent to the chapter-wise PDF library. When the PDF library changes, keep the generated counts in `src/data/netGsetStats.js` as the single source of truth.

### 3. Mehsana local discovery
Local pages should stay truthful and focused on services actually offered. The primary local hub is `/commerce-coaching-mehsana`, supported by board/class/subject landing pages such as CBSE Commerce, Class 11 Commerce, Class 12 Commerce, Economics, Business Studies and GSEB Economics in Mehsana.

Do not create near-identical locality pages for every neighbourhood merely to rank. Add a new local landing page only when it represents a genuinely different service or useful student need.

### 4. Long-tail student questions
Prefer pages that solve one real student problem well: numericals, diagrams, case studies, important questions, revision plans, chapter mistakes, calculators and exam-specific concepts. Existing authority guides, silent-search pages and genuine-traffic pages already provide this structure.

## Weekly workflow

1. Check Google Search Console for pages gaining impressions but receiving few clicks.
2. Improve titles/descriptions only when the search intent and page content genuinely match.
3. Add internal links from a strong relevant page to an important page that is difficult to discover.
4. Publish or upgrade one useful resource rather than several thin pages.
5. Check Search Console indexing for newly published strategic URLs.
6. Review GA4 landing pages to see which organic pages lead students deeper into notes, quizzes, enquiries or premium resources.

## Monthly workflow

- Review top queries and landing pages.
- Look for pages ranking around positions 4–20 with meaningful impressions; improve the page before creating a competing URL.
- Check sitemap and canonical consistency.
- Test important pages on mobile.
- Review Google Business Profile accuracy, genuine reviews, photos and website links.
- Find one legitimate educational backlink opportunity: an authored article, teacher profile, resource citation, school/education directory listing or genuinely useful tool that another site may reference.

## CTR rules

Titles should lead with the student's search intent, then a concrete benefit or proof point when accurate. Examples:
- `UGC NET Commerce Notes — 43 Free PDFs, All 10 Units`
- `GSET Commerce Code 17 Notes — 43 Free PDFs`
- `Economics Tuition in Mehsana | Class 11 & 12 + Free Demo`

Do not change a ranking page's title repeatedly. Give search engines time to recrawl and measure the result.

## Internal-link architecture

Use a predictable path:

`Homepage → Study Material / Exam Hub → Subject or Unit → Chapter Resource → Practice / Related Resource`

Every unit or chapter page should link back to its parent hub and to the next logical learning step.

## Backlink rules

Good backlinks are earned because the destination is useful or the author genuinely contributed something. Prioritise:
- educational contributor articles with a relevant author bio;
- real teacher or tutoring profiles;
- reputable local/business citations with consistent details;
- resource citations to calculators, notes or original teaching guides;
- relevant school, college or education communities where a resource is actually useful.

Avoid paid link packages, automated directory blasts, link exchanges at scale, private blog networks and fake guest posts.

## Google Business Profile

Use accurate services, genuine student/parent reviews, current photos and a consistent website link. Do not keyword-stuff the business name or manufacture reviews. Local rankings depend on relevance, distance and prominence; the website can strengthen relevance but cannot control a searcher's physical distance.

## Measurement

Track four separate outcomes instead of one vanity traffic number:

1. Search impressions — Google is discovering the site.
2. Organic clicks — snippets and rankings are producing visits.
3. Engaged landing-page sessions — visitors are finding the page useful.
4. Meaningful actions — PDF opens, quiz starts, demo/contact actions or legitimate purchases.

A traffic increase that does not help students reach useful resources is not the goal.

## Safe growth rule

Do not rename the domain, mass-delete indexed URLs, change canonical structure or publish hundreds of generated pages without a migration and rollback plan. Improve the existing topical authority first: Commerce, CBSE/GSEB, UGC NET/GSET and Mehsana.
