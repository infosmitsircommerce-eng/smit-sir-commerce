Build your chapter revision routine

Read the textbook topic and the chapter notes together. Write down the meaning of each economic term, then organise causes, effects and measures under separate headings. For a difference question, compare the two concepts using the same basis in each row. For an explanation question, start with the definition and support it with the chapter's relevant points.

After reading, close the PDF and attempt the chapter practice questions from memory. Check your answer for missing keywords and revise the specific section again. The practice collection remains free, including Population and Agriculture Sector while their new free PDFs are awaited.
