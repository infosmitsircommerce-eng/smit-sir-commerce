Levels, scoring and sources

Easy: concept identification. Moderate: matching, assertion–reason and two application statements. Hard: three advanced case statements and multi-step numericals. Extreme: five advanced case statements and longer numerical decisions. The levels are editorial estimates; your experience may differ.

Correct: 2 marks. Wrong or unanswered: 0. Unit-test time limits are practice settings. The 100-question subject mocks allow 120 minutes; Paper 1 is separate. Combined tests contain 25 Moderate and 25 Hard questions, with five from each unit. Equal unit distribution is a practice choice, not a prediction of exam weightage.

These are original practice questions, not reproduced official papers. Concepts and application cases recur across levels; mixed mocks reuse selected unit questions. A complete review of every NET/GSET paper from 2016–2025 has not been verified.

Reference book supplied by Smit Sir: KVS Madaan, UGC NET Commerce Paper 2, with its 2021–2023 paper sections. Unit explanations use original wording. Tax questions specify historical law or the relevant transition context.
